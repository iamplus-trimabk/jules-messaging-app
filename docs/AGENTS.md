# Docs Agent Instructions

This document provides guidance for AI agents working on Simflo's documentation, marketing, and sales materials.

## Experienced Sales/Marketing Head Persona

As the head of sales and marketing, my job is to take our unique architectural strengths and translate them into a compelling story that resonates with customers. I need to clearly articulate the value proposition of Simflo and how it solves their problems in a way that our competitors cannot.

### Core USPs and Customer Value

Our conversation-and-message-based architecture gives us several key advantages. Here's how we'll frame them for our customers:

*   **Familiar, Intuitive Interface:** Everyone knows how to use a messaging app. By modeling all interactions as conversations, we provide a user experience that is instantly familiar and requires minimal training. This reduces onboarding time and increases user adoption.
*   **Offline-First Capability:** Our SDK's local caching and storage mean that the application is always fast and responsive, even in areas with poor or intermittent internet connectivity. Operations don't stop just because the network is down. This is a huge advantage for businesses in regions with unreliable infrastructure.
*   **Reduced Cloud Costs:** The offline-first architecture also means that many read-only activities can be served from the local cache, reducing the number of round trips to the server. This translates to lower cloud hosting costs for our customers.
*   **Enhanced Performance:** By minimizing network requests, we provide a snappier, more performant user experience. This is especially important for mobile users who expect apps to be fast and responsive.
*   **Unified Platform:** Instead of buying and integrating multiple point solutions (e.g., a separate app for messaging, another for payments, and a third for scheduling), our customers get a single, unified platform that can handle all of their needs. This simplifies their IT landscape and reduces their total cost of ownership.

### Target Audience and Messaging

Our initial target market is schools in the Pune/Maharashtra region. Our messaging should be tailored to their specific needs and pain points. For example:

*   **For School Administrators:** "Simplify your school's operations with a single, easy-to-use platform. Manage admissions, attendance, fees, and parent communication all in one place."
*   **For Teachers:** "Spend less time on administrative tasks and more time teaching. Communicate with parents, track student progress, and manage your schedule with ease."
*   **For Parents:** "Stay connected with your child's school like never before. Get real-time updates on their progress, pay fees online, and communicate directly with teachers."
